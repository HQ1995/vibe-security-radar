security: Raise error when jsonschema unavailable instead of silently passing (GHSA-425g-fjhq-5h92)

validate_against_schema() now raises JSONValidationError when
jsonschema is not installed, instead of silently returning (which
pretended validation succeeded).

Changed import-time print() warning to logging.warning() for proper
log integration. jsonschema is already listed as a required dependency
in requirements.txt.
